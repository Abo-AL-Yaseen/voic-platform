- Al-Kawakib vegetable ghee: 2 kg tin, 15 kg tin, and 15 kg drum.
- Al-Nabatin: 850 g, 1.7 kg, and 15 kg.
- Al-Balqa: confirmed weight of 15 kg.
- Al-Ghazal Butter Flavor: 455 g.
- Al-Mumtaz: 15 kg tin.
- Al-Aseel: 10 kg and 12.5 kg cartons.
- Al-Kawkab soybean oil: 16 kg PET.
